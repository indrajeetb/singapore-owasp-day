Requirements

Submission Requirements

  • Provide a detailed training outline covering learning objectives, session structure, and lab design.
  • Specify all technical requirements (VMs, lab environments, software). We will communicate these to students in advance.
  • Workshops must be predominantly hands-on. Lecture-only formats will not be accepted.
  • Include your trainer bio and any relevant credentials or prior training experience.
  • Specify the target audience and any prerequisites (e.g., "basic Python", "familiarity with Burp Suite").
  • All training content must be in English.
  • Content must be your original work or material you are licensed to teach.
  • Trainers receive a share of training ticket revenue. Minimum student numbers may apply.
  • Trainers provide their own lab infrastructure (cloud-based labs preferred).
