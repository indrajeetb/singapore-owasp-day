Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can submit?

Anyone. We welcome researchers, practitioners, engineers, consultants, and academics. First-time speakers are encouraged.

Does the talk need to be about OWASP projects?

Not at all. Any topic related to application security, web, API, AI, cloud, or DevSecOps security is welcome.

Can I submit multiple proposals?

Yes. Each submission is reviewed independently on its merits.

How do I contact the organising team?

Email indrajeet.bhuyan@owasp.org with questions about your submission.
