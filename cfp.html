Requirements

Submission Guidelines

  • Submissions must be original content not previously presented at other Singapore conferences in 2026.
  • Talks must be non-commercial. Vendor pitches will not be accepted.
  • Abstract should be 300–500 words with clear learning objectives and target audience.
  • Include a short bio (150 words max) and profile photo.
  • First-time speakers are welcome — we provide preparation support.
  • All presentations must be delivered in English.
  • Live demos are encouraged. Practical, technical content is strongly preferred.
  • Accepted speakers receive complimentary conference admission for themselves and one guest.
  • Final slides must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the event.
  • Talks may be recorded and published on the OWASP Singapore YouTube channel (consent required).
