About Singapore OWASP Day

Who We Are

The OWASP Singapore Chapter

The OWASP Singapore Chapter is a community of cybersecurity practitioners, developers, and researchers dedicated to improving application security in Singapore and the broader Asia-Pacific region.

Founded in 2008, the chapter has grown into one of the most active OWASP chapters in Asia — hosting regular meetups, workshops, training sessions, and the annual AppSec Days Singapore conference.

The chapter brings together professionals from government, finance, technology, and academia to share knowledge, advance best practices, and champion a culture of security-first development.

In 2026, we level up with Singapore OWASP Day — a flagship event that is Bigger, Broader, and Bolder than anything we've done before.

History

Our Journey

Over 17 years of growing Singapore's security community — one meetup, workshop, and conference at a time.

2007
Singapore Security Meetup Group Founded

A small gathering of security enthusiasts at GeekTerminal on 15 November 2007 sparked what would become one of Asia's most active security communities. Founding members included people who would later go on to work at Apple, Google Mandiant, and the Cyber Security Agency of Singapore.

2008
OWASP Singapore Chapter Officially Established

The chapter held its first official meeting on 29 May 2008 at JCU @ Bukit Merah, with presentations on WebScarab (predecessor to ZAP Proxy) and Burp Suite. From less than 20 members, the community began to grow rapidly.

2010
Regular Meetups, Workshops & Training

Regular chapter meetups, workshops, and training sessions began running across Singapore — covering venues including Cairnhill CC and various universities and tech hubs.

2023
AppSec Days Singapore Launched

We launched AppSec Days Singapore as our dedicated annual conference — a focused event bringing together application security professionals from across the region.

2024
200+ Participants · 8+ Countries · CTF at Marina Bay Sands

OWASP AppSec Days Singapore 2024 attracted over 200 conference participants from 8+ countries, hosted 2 training courses, and featured an exciting CTF competition at Marina Bay Sands.

2025
250+ Participants · 12+ Countries · Lifelong Learning Institute & M Hotel

Growing to 250+ conference participants from 12+ countries, OWASP AppSec Days Singapore 2025 delivered world-class security content across Lifelong Learning Institute and M Hotel, with 2 training courses and a CTF competition.

2026
Singapore OWASP Day — Bigger, Broader, Bolder!

Our most ambitious event yet. Singapore OWASP Day 2026 at SIT Punggol is set to welcome 200–300 participants from across Asia-Pacific for Training Day, CTF Competition, and Conference Day. Join us.

Our Foundation

About OWASP

The Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) is a nonprofit foundation dedicated to improving software security. All OWASP tools, documents, forums, and chapters are free and open to anyone interested in improving application security.

OWASP is known for producing free, community-developed resources including the OWASP Top 10, OWASP Web Security Testing Guide, OWASP ASVS, and many more projects trusted by developers and security professionals worldwide.
