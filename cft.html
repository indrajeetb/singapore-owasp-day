Requirements

Submission Requirements

  • Provide a detailed training outline covering learning objectives, session structure, and lab/exercise design.
  • Specify all technical requirements (VMs, lab environments, software, hardware). We will communicate requirements to registered students in advance.
  • Workshops must be predominantly hands-on. Lecture-only formats will not be accepted for the training day.
  • Include your trainer bio and any relevant credentials, certifications, or prior training experience.
  • Specify the target audience and any prerequisites (e.g., "basic Python", "familiarity with Burp Suite").
  • All training content must be in English.
  • Content must be your original work or material you are licensed to teach.
  • Accepted trainers receive a share of training ticket revenue. Minimum student numbers may apply per class.
  • Trainers are responsible for providing their own lab infrastructure (cloud-based labs preferred). We can assist with logistics.
  • OWASP Singapore may feature your training abstract on the website for marketing purposes. Notify us if any content is confidential.
