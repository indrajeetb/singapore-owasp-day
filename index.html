Location

Conference Venue

Venue Sponsor Singapore Institute of Technology

Singapore Institute of Technology

 Punggol District, Singapore 828608

The Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) is Singapore's first university of applied learning. Located in the vibrant Punggol District, the state-of-the-art campus features modern lecture theatres, hackathon spaces, and collaborative environments — making it the ideal setting for SGOD 2026.

Easily accessible by MRT (Punggol Station, NE17/PTC) and bus. Surrounding amenities include food courts, cafes, and hotels.

Programme

Three Tracks, Two Days

A packed two-day programme of world-class security content across training, competition, and conference formats.

Training Day

Intensive hands-on workshops led by industry experts. Half-day and full-day formats covering practical offensive and defensive security techniques.

CTF Competition

Test your skills in our Capture The Flag challenge. Open to all experience levels — from beginners to seasoned professionals. Prizes for top teams.

Conference Day

Keynotes, technical talks, and panels from top security practitioners across Asia-Pacific and beyond. Networking opportunities throughout.
