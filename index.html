Location

Conference Venue

Singapore Institute of Technology

  Punggol District, Singapore 828608

The Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) is Singapore's first university of applied learning. Located in the vibrant Punggol District, the state-of-the-art campus features modern lecture theatres, hackathon spaces, and collaborative learning environments — making it the perfect setting for SGOD 2026.

Easily accessible by MRT (Punggol Station, NE17/PTC) and bus. Ample surrounding amenities including food courts, cafes, and hotels.

Programme

What to Expect

Three tracks over two days packed with world-class security content.

Training Day

Intensive hands-on workshops led by industry experts. Half-day and full-day formats covering practical offensive and defensive security techniques.

CTF Competition

Test your skills in our Capture The Flag challenge. Open to all experience levels — beginners to seasoned professionals. Prizes for top teams.

Conference Day

Keynotes, technical talks, and panels from top security practitioners across the Asia-Pacific region and beyond. Networking opportunities throughout.
