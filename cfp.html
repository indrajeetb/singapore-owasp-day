Formats

Talk Formats

30 min

Focused presentation with 5 minutes for Q&A. Ideal for sharing a specific finding, tool, technique, or case study.

Requirements

Submission Guidelines

  • Submissions must be original content not previously presented at other conferences in Singapore in the same calendar year.
  • Talks must be non-commercial. Vendor pitches are not accepted. Mentioning your employer or open source tools is fine.
  • Abstract should be 300–500 words, clearly describing the topic, key takeaways, and target audience.
  • Include a short speaker bio (150 words max) and a profile photo in your submission.
  • Indicate whether you are a first-time speaker — our team provides coaching and preparation support.
  • All presentations must be in English.
  • Live demos are encouraged — practical, technical content is strongly preferred.
  • Accepted speakers receive complimentary conference admission for the speaker and one guest.
  • Slides must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the event for review.
  • Recordings of talks may be published on the OWASP Singapore YouTube channel. You will be asked to confirm consent.
