Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can submit a talk or training?

Anyone! We welcome researchers, practitioners, engineers, consultants, and academics. First-time speakers are encouraged — we have a supportive reviewing team.

Does the talk need to be about OWASP projects?

Not at all. We welcome any topic related to application security, web security, API security, AI security, cloud security, DevSecOps, and related fields.

Can I submit multiple proposals?

Yes, you can submit multiple abstracts. Each will be reviewed independently on its merits.

What is the review process?

Submissions are reviewed by the OWASP Singapore programme committee. Decisions are based on topic relevance, originality, depth, and speaker credibility. Results announced 30 July 2026.

How do I contact the organising team?

Reach out to us at ocwong@owasp.org or cecil.su@owasp.org with any questions about your submission.
